# KYRO help

KYRO pays creators commission on the sales their videos drive. Brands run
campaigns, creators upload videos, and KYRO matches every Shopify order back to
the video behind it.

Questions this page doesn't answer: **contact@itskyro.com**.

---

## For brands

### 1. Connect your store

Install KYRO from the Shopify App Store. Shopify asks you to approve access,
then sends you to KYRO to create your account. Your store is connected
automatically.

KYRO reads your orders, products and fulfillments. It never sees your
customers' names, email addresses, phone numbers or addresses.

### 2. Finish setup

Add your website, brand name and logo. That's what creators see when they
browse campaigns.

### 3. Create a campaign

A campaign says what you want made and what it pays:

- **Products** with images, prices and links. Add as many as the campaign covers.
- **Commission**, either a percentage of each order or a flat amount per order.
- **Deliverable**, for example "1 vertical video, 15 to 30 seconds".
- **Brief**, the tone, the hooks that work for you, what to avoid.

### 4. Get creators

Two ways, and you can use both:

- **Invite link.** Copy it from the Creators page and send it to creators you
  already work with. The link skips the application queue: whoever opens it is
  on the campaign.
- **Applications.** Creators on KYRO can find your campaign and apply. You see
  their profile and pitch, then accept or decline. Declining asks you for a
  reason, which the creator sees, so they can come back with something closer.

### 5. Decide what runs

Creators upload without waiting for approval, so content arrives fast. In
**Submissions** you preview each video and either use it or pass on it. Passing
asks for a note, and that note starts a private thread with the creator about
that one video.

You can download the videos you're using, with the campaign filter applied.

### 6. Track and pay

The dashboard shows campaigns, videos, attributed orders and impressions. Every
number opens the rows behind it.

**Finance** shows what you owe, what's still clearing, and what you've paid.
Commission becomes payable once an order clears its return window, which is 30
days by default. That window protects you from paying on an order that's
refunded a week later.

Pay by bank debit or by card. Bank is cheaper to process, so it's what KYRO
recommends.

### 7. Add your team

Settings, then Team. Invite teammates by email and pick a role: Admin, Manager,
Creator manager or Viewer. Permissions are adjustable per person, so "runs
campaigns but cannot move money" is one click.

## For creators

### Getting on a campaign

Browse open campaigns, or open an invite link from a brand. An invite link puts
you straight on the campaign. An application goes to the brand, and you'll see
whether they accepted, or why they passed.

### Uploading

Pick the campaign, upload the video, done. No approval needed before you post.
The brand then marks it in use or not used, and if they pass, they have to tell
you why. You can reply and ask.

### Getting paid

You earn commission on orders your video drove. An order has to clear its
return window before the money is yours, so earnings move from pending, to
clearing, to available. Available earnings can be withdrawn.

Your orders page shows every sale credited to you: the campaign, the date, the
commission, and where it is in that cycle.

## Pricing

**Free for your first 30 days.**

After that, KYRO charges **1% of attributed sales**, billed on your Shopify
bill. Attributed sales means the product subtotal of orders KYRO credited to
creator content, after discounts, excluding shipping and tax, minus refunds.

The fee is never stacked. There is no charge on ad spend, no charge on creator
earnings, and no monthly platform fee.

Creator commission is separate. That's money you pay your own creators, at the
rate you set on each campaign, and KYRO keeps none of it.

## How attribution works

1. Each creator video gets a tracking code.
2. The code travels in the ad link as `utm_content`.
3. A shopper clicks the ad and buys.
4. Shopify tells KYRO about the order, and KYRO looks at how that shopper
   arrived. If the tracking code is there, the order is credited to that
   creator.

Orders with no KYRO code are ignored. You are never charged for sales your
creators didn't drive.

## Common questions

**Do I need a Meta ad account?**
No. Attribution runs on Shopify orders and the tracking link, so you can run
ads however you like. Connecting Meta adds one-click publishing and ad stats.

**What if an order is refunded?**
Refunds come off the commissionable value, and a refund inside the clearing
window means the commission never becomes payable.

**Can a creator upload before I approve them?**
Creators upload only to campaigns they are on. Once they're on, no per-video
approval is needed. You decide afterwards what runs.

**Can I change the commission on a live campaign?**
Yes, and the rate is recorded per order, so changing it never rewrites what a
creator has already earned.

**What data does KYRO store from my store?**
Order id and number, totals, status, line items, and the marketing parameters
on the visit. No customer names, emails, phone numbers or addresses.

**How do I disconnect?**
Uninstall KYRO from your Shopify admin. The access token is deleted
immediately, and store data is erased when Shopify asks us to, 48 hours later.
Earnings already owed to creators are kept, because they're a record of money
owed.
