# KYRO — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

[ENTITY] ("KYRO," "we," "us") operates the KYRO platform at [DOMAIN]. This policy explains
what we collect, why, who we share it with, and what you can do about it.

It covers three groups: **Brands** (businesses using KYRO), **Creators** (people producing
video), and **Customers** of connected stores, whose order data reaches us through a
Brand's Shopify connection. If you are a Customer, §4 and §9 are the parts about you.

---

## 1. Information you give us

**Everyone:** name, email address, password (stored hashed, never in readable form),
and anything you write to us in support conversations.

**Creators:** profile details you choose to provide — handle, bio, location, niche, social
handles and follower counts — the videos and images you upload, and the tax and payout
information required to pay you (see §5).

**Brands:** company name, brand profile, campaign briefs and settings, and billing details
(see §5).

## 2. Information from connected platforms

You choose whether to connect these. Disconnecting stops the flow.

**Meta (Facebook and Instagram).** When a Brand connects an ad account we receive ad
account identifiers, campaign and ad structure, and performance metrics: impressions,
clicks, spend, and conversions Meta attributes. When a Creator grants partnership
permissions we receive the identifiers needed to run partnership ads from their handle.

**Shopify.** When a Brand connects a store we receive product and inventory data, order
records, and fulfilment and refund events. See §4 for what that means for Customers.

## 3. Information collected automatically

Device and browser type, IP address, pages visited, and actions taken in the app. We use
this to keep the service running, diagnose problems, and detect abuse.

## 4. Customer order data (important)

To calculate what a Brand owes and what a Creator has earned, we receive order records
from the Brand's connected Shopify store. Those records can include a Customer's name,
email address, shipping address, order contents, order value, and refund status.

**What we do with it:** attribute the order to the advertising that drove it, calculate
commission, and detect fraud such as self-purchasing.

**What we do not do with it:** we do not sell it, we do not use it to market to Customers,
we do not build advertising profiles from it, and we do not share it with Creators.
Creators see that an order occurred and what they earned. They do not see who bought.

**Roles.** For this data the Brand is the controller and KYRO is a processor acting on the
Brand's instructions. If you are a Customer and want your data accessed or deleted, contact
the store you purchased from; we will act on their instruction, and you can also reach us
directly at [PRIVACY_EMAIL].

## 5. Payment and tax information

We do not store full card numbers or bank credentials. Payment details are collected and
held by our payment processor. We keep a token, the last four digits, and the status of
transactions.

For Creator payouts, our payout provider collects tax identification and the W-9 or W-8BEN
required before a first payout, and files 1099-NEC forms where required. We receive
confirmation of status and the amounts paid, not the underlying tax identifiers.

## 6. How we use information

To operate the platform: run campaigns, match Creators to Brands, publish approved videos
as ads, track performance, calculate and settle commission, and process payouts. To
communicate with you about your account, campaigns and earnings. To provide support. To
detect fraud, abuse and self-dealing. To improve the service. To meet legal, tax and
accounting obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral
advertising.

## 7. Who we share it with

**Service providers**, each handling data only to provide their service to us:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Supabase | Authentication, database and file storage |
| Vercel | Application hosting |
| Resend | Transactional email |
| Meta | Ad delivery and performance |
| Shopify | Product and order data from connected stores |
| Payment processor | Brand billing (ACH and card) |
| Payout provider | Creator payouts and tax forms |

**Between users, by design.** A Creator's profile and submitted videos are visible to
Brands running campaigns they participate in. A Brand's campaign details are visible to
Creators considering it. Earnings and billing figures are visible to the parties to that
transaction.

**Legal and safety.** Where required by law, or to protect our rights, users, or the
public.

**Business transfer.** If KYRO is acquired or merges, information may transfer as part of
that transaction. We will give notice before your information becomes subject to a
different privacy policy.

## 8. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies that are necessary to sign you in and keep you
signed in, remember preferences such as light or dark theme, and understand aggregate
usage. We do not use third-party advertising cookies on our own site. Your browser can
block cookies, though sign-in will not work without the necessary ones.

## 9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive
a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to
withdraw consent.

**Account holders** can update most information in Settings, disconnect Meta or Shopify at
any time, and request deletion of the account by writing to [PRIVACY_EMAIL]. We respond
within 45 days.

**California residents.** Under the CCPA/CPRA you have the rights above plus the right not
to be discriminated against for exercising them. We do not sell personal information or
share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is no opt-out to offer. You may
designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf.

**Customers of connected stores.** See §4 — direct requests to the store you bought from,
or contact us and we will coordinate.

Some information is retained after a deletion request where we are required to keep it, for
example transaction and tax records.

## 10. Retention

Account information is kept while your account is open and for a reasonable period after,
to handle disputes and meet obligations. Financial and tax records are kept as long as tax
and accounting law requires, typically seven years. Order data used for attribution is
kept for the life of the campaign plus the period needed to settle and audit it.
Uploaded video is kept while any licence to it remains in force.

## 11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted to those who need it.
Passwords are hashed. Database access is governed by row-level rules so accounts can only
reach their own records. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute
security, but we will notify affected users and regulators of a breach as the law requires.

## 12. Shopify

Where KYRO is installed on a Shopify store, we handle store and customer data in line with
Shopify's requirements for app developers, we request only the scopes needed to read
products, orders and fulfilment events, and we stop accessing store data when the app is
uninstalled. Data received from a store is deleted or anonymised within 90 days of
uninstall unless we must retain it for financial or legal reasons.

## 13. International transfers

We operate in the United States and our providers may process data there and elsewhere. If
you use KYRO from outside the US, you understand your information will be transferred to
and processed in the US, and we rely on appropriate safeguards where required.

## 14. Children

KYRO is for people 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under
18. If we learn we have, we delete it.

## 15. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified at least 14 days before they
take effect, and the effective date above will change.
