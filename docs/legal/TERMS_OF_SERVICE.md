## Part A, General

### 1. What KYRO does

KYRO connects Brands with Creators. Creators produce short-form video. Brands approve it
and run it as paid advertising in their own Meta ad account under partnership permissions.
KYRO tracks the orders that advertising drives through the Brand's connected Shopify store
and settles commission between Brand and Creator.

**KYRO does not buy media.** Advertising runs in the Brand's own Meta ad account and is
billed to the Brand by Meta directly. Nothing in these Terms makes KYRO responsible for
the Brand's advertising costs.

### 2. Accounts

You must be 18 or older. You are responsible for your account credentials and for activity
under your account. Provide accurate information and keep it current. One person or entity
per account unless we agree otherwise in writing.

### 3. Definitions

- **Campaign**, a Brand's program with a stated commission rate, brief and deliverables.
- **Submission**, a video a Creator uploads to a Campaign.
- **Approved Video**, a Submission the Brand has accepted.
- **Connected Accounts**, the Brand's Meta ad account and Shopify store, linked to KYRO.
- **Attributed Order**, an order recorded in the Connected Shopify store that KYRO
  attributes to an Approved Video under the attribution rule published for that Campaign.
- **Commissionable Value**, product subtotal, after discounts, **excluding** shipping,
  taxes and duties, and **net of** refunds and chargebacks.
- **Creator Commission**, the percentage or amount the Brand sets for a Campaign, applied
  to Commissionable Value (or to ad spend or a flat fee, where the Campaign says so).
- **KYRO Fee**, 1%, as described in §8.
- **Payment Run**, one authorised payment covering every Attributed Order that has
  reached Available and not yet been paid for. One charge to the Brand, one transfer to
  each Creator it covers.
- **Due Date**, the date Creator Commission on an Attributed Order becomes payable, being
  30 days after that Order reaches Available.
- **Processing Cost**, the fee the payment processor charges to take a payment by the
  method you chose. It is added to the amount charged and paid to the processor in full.
  KYRO retains no part of it and adds no margin to it.

Disconnecting either Connected Account, or revoking KYRO's access to it, **suspends your
Campaigns and suspends every license granted under §9** until access is restored.

### 6. No deposit and no prepayment

**KYRO does not hold a deposit and does not take money before orders exist.** There is no
security deposit, no prepaid balance, no media budget held by KYRO, and nothing to fund
before a Campaign can open.

You pay for Creator Commission on Attributed Orders that have already cleared their return
window, plus the KYRO Fee on those same orders, and nothing else. A Campaign that drives
no attributed orders costs you nothing beyond the advertising Meta bills you directly.

An earlier version of these Terms required a refundable security deposit of $2,500 and
billed against it. That is no longer how KYRO works and no deposit is or will be collected

### 7. Billing and payment authorization

**You decide when to pay, and you authorize the charge when you do.** KYRO does not charge

You authorize KYRO to charge the payment method on file for the full amount of a Payment

**When payment is due.** Creator Commission on an Attributed Order becomes payable on its

Amounts overdue accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum the law allows, whichever

**KYRO does not advance money to Creators.** KYRO transfers to a Creator only what it has

video is conditional on your paying it.

### 8. KYRO Fee

KYRO charges **1% of Attributed Sales**. Campaigns pay Creators on sales commission, so
there is one base. The KYRO Fee is the only amount KYRO charges you and the only amount
KYRO keeps. There is no subscription, no seat fee, no listing fee and no success fee on
top of it.

**The KYRO Fee is charged to you, not to the Creator.** It is billed alongside Creator
Commission on the same cycle, and nothing is deducted from what a Creator earns.

**Processing Cost is passed through at cost.** Taking a payment costs money, and the
amount depends on the method you choose. That cost is added to the amount charged and
paid to the processor in full. KYRO retains no part of it and adds no margin to it, which
is why it appears as its own line rather than inside the KYRO Fee.

Paying by bank account costs materially less to process than paying by card. The exact
amount for your chosen method is shown in your dashboard, itemised, **before** you
authorize a payment, and it is recorded against the payment afterwards. If the processor
changes its rates, the change applies to payments made after that date and never
retroactively to a payment already authorized.

**30-day free trial for Brands that install from the Shopify App Store.** If you first came
to KYRO by installing it from the Shopify App Store, no KYRO Fee is charged on orders placed
in your first 30 days. The KYRO Fee applies as described above to orders placed after that.
Creator Commission is payable throughout.

### 14. Earnings

Commission accrues on Attributed Orders at the rate the Campaign states, applied to
Commissionable Value. Your earnings move through four states:

| State | When | Payable to you |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** | The order is placed | No |
| **Clearing** | The order is fulfilled | No |
| **Available** | 30 days after fulfilment | **Yes, once the Brand pays** |
| **Reversed** | The order is refunded or charged back | Removed |

Pending and Clearing amounts are **estimates of orders that have not yet cleared**. They
are shown live so you can see performance as it happens, but they are not money you hold,
and they can be reduced if orders are refunded or charged back. Reversals are always shown
explicitly.

If a reversal occurs after an amount has been paid to you, we may offset it against future
earnings.

**Available means the Brand owes it, not that KYRO is holding it.** KYRO does not hold your
money and does not pay you out of its own funds. An Available amount is commission the
Brand owes and has not yet paid. It reaches you when they authorize a Payment Run, which
they may do at any time and must do by the Due Date in §7. If a Brand does not pay, the
licence they hold in your video terminates automatically under §9(e) and their continued
use of it is copyright infringement you can act on.

**Your bank details are held by the payment provider, not by KYRO.** You enter them during
